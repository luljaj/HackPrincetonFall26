# Draft Dora curriculum

This roadmap uses the skills and prepared moments in `apps/web/app/lab/mastery.json`, including the three addition moments used by the TV. Grade bands describe a provisional teaching sequence, not an assessment of the child's school grade. They need educator review.

| Subject | Pre-K | Kindergarten | Grade 1 / next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Numbers | Count to 5 | Count to 10; say how many | Add small numbers; plan addition/subtraction within 10 |
| Shapes | Name shapes | Match a shape to its opening | Plan building and comparing shapes |
| Remembering | Recall an object | Recall a recent event | Recall places in order; plan a three-event retelling |
| Choosing | Pick an object | Choose a path/place | Plan explaining and comparing choices |
| Words | Name objects | Plan action words | Plan descriptions and a new word in a sentence |
| Stories | Imagine and describe | Predict what happens next | Plan feelings and explaining a character's choice |
| Joining in | Answer aloud | Join a chant or action | Plan leading an activity |
| Episode Spanish | Repeat the episode's Spanish words | Plan short phrases | Expand after new lessons are prepared |

Each subject has its own vertical track, topic checkpoints, and grade separators. Checkpoint numbers are positions on the track, not percentages or grades. Grey nodes either await earlier mastery or need an authored lesson; the label distinguishes these cases. Existing saved activity remains visible even if an earlier checkpoint is incomplete.

Mastery comes from the saved assessment rules, never from the roadmap or Luna's prose. Practising fills part of the flower without marking a skill mastered. Resets keep the original evidence. Parent-set language levels (New, Heard, Said, Remembered) are explicitly labelled and stored separately for each language and lesson; hiding Spanish while Hindi is selected does not erase Spanish.

Luna explains saved evidence and suggests one teaching step. It cannot change scores. Older TV answers are included; sample fixtures are excluded. The retired `/lab` experience is outside this data work.
